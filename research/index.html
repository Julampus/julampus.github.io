Research

Philosophy

I take particular interest in the questions raised by feminist philosophy, the philosophy of science, and phenomenology. My educational background and many of my works are centered around these topics.

Natural Sciences

I am trained in molecular techniques as they are applied to cell and organismal biology, though I intend to explore all aspects of the biological sciences. My research spans protein engineering, sexual behavior, and theoretical science.

Experience

2026 - Present

Axel Lab - The Zuckerman Institute

Currently, I am surveying and characterizing the mating behavior of the dwarf cuttlefish. Their fascinating reproductive biology offers up countless questions of inquiry, including:

  • What are the mechanisms (social, chemical, mechanical) by which cuttlefish initiate mating?
  • How are sperm stored via. the female cuttlefish
  • Can we use what we have gathered to develop IVF and cryopreservation protocols?

In elucidating the mystery behind its mating, this research can potentially inform future inquiries into the dwarf cuttlefish model orgnaism.


2024 - 2025

Freeman Lab - The University of Minnesota

My research at the freeman lab centered around the characterization of a single peptide from the Ribosomally-synthesized, Post-translationally modified Peptide (RiPP) family. This "borosin" family is associated to various enzymes, including a protease and methyltransferase, that all aid in the synthesis of a final natural product. This research helped confirm various key steps in the synthesis of this peptide and prompts future research into the biological wellspring of natural products.


2023 - 2024

Wagner Lab - The University of Minnesota

At the Wagner Lab, I studied and synthesized nanobodies engineered for the lab's Chemically Self-Assembling Nanoring immunotherapy. While short-chain variable fragments, another type of antibody, were previously used in order to acheive cancer cell ligand specificity, their insolubility and large size made proved difficult to work with. Nanobodies would address these concerns and could expodite research into this emerging immunotherapy.
