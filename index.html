About

Juniper Setterberg is an undergraduate student at Barnard College interested in philosophy and biology. She is currently working with the Axel Lab at Columbia University's Zuckerman Institute. Her research revolves around characterizing mating behaviors of the dwarf cuttlefish Ascarosepion bandense. Apart from her academic interests, Juniper is a classically trained cellist, predominantly performing early 20th century pieces.
